Does Size Matter?

The happiest countries in the world are small. Is that a coincidence?
A data-driven journey exploring the relationship between population size, growth, and national happiness.

Scroll to explore
01 — The Observation

Small Countries,
Big Smiles

When we look at the World Happiness Report, a striking pattern emerges: the countries that consistently rank highest — Finland, Denmark, Iceland, Norway — are all relatively small nations. Let's put this on a chart.

💡 Key Insight

There appears to be a negative correlation between population size and happiness score. Smaller countries tend to cluster at the top of the happiness rankings.

02 — The Exploration

How Has
Population Growth
Shaped Happiness?

Population doesn't just sit still — it grows, sometimes rapidly. Countries with rapid population growth face different challenges: more pressure on infrastructure, education, healthcare, and governance.

Here we compare the population trajectories of some of the happiest and least happy nations over the past 60 years.

💡 Key Insight

The happiest countries tend to have slow, stable population growth, while many of the least happy countries have seen rapid population increases.

03 — The Nuance

It's Not
Just About Size

Population size alone doesn't determine happiness. Factors like GDP per capita, social support, and personal freedom play crucial roles — and these tend to be stronger in smaller nations.

Explore how different factors correlate with happiness across regions. Select an axis variable and hover to discover each country's story.

💡 Key Insight

GDP per capita and social support show much stronger correlations with happiness than population size — suggesting that how a country is governed matters more than how many people live there.

04 — Conclusion

What Really Makes
A Nation Happy?

Our journey through the data reveals that while smaller countries do tend to be happier, size itself is not the cause. Rather, smaller nations often share traits — strong social safety nets, higher GDP per capita, greater personal freedom — that are the true drivers of well-being.

🏘️

Small ≠ Happy

Population size correlates with happiness, but doesn't cause it. Many small countries also score poorly.

📈

Growth Matters

Rapid population growth strains resources and governance, making it harder to maintain quality of life.

🏛️

Governance Wins

GDP, social support, freedom, and low corruption — these are the real predictors of national happiness.
